## App name (30 max)

DirectServe: Cast & File Share

## Short description (80 max)

Cast to TV, mirror your screen and transfer files over WiFi. No app needed.

## Full description (4000 max)

Show your phone's videos, photos, music and files on any screen nearby — a TV, a laptop, a tablet or another phone — without cables, accounts or uploading anything.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose what to share.
2. Tap Start sharing.
3. Scan the QR code (or type the short address) on the other device.
That's it. The other device only needs a web browser. No app to install there.

PLAY AND DOWNLOAD IN THE BROWSER
• A home page like your own streaming app: a featured video, rows to swipe through, and Continue watching that picks up where you stopped.
• Videos play straight from your phone. When a browser can't play a file as it is, DirectServe makes a compatible version when you press Play.
• Photos open full size, with next and previous.
• Music plays right in the page.
• Download single files, everything, or a ZIP.

SEND FILES BACK TO YOUR PHONE
Anyone in the session can send photos, videos and files to your phone from their browser. You approve each transfer first.

MIRROR YOUR SCREEN
Show your phone's screen live in a browser, with sound from apps that allow capture. Good for presentations, demos and showing photos to a room.

SEND TEXT AND LINKS
Paste a link or a note on the laptop and it pops up on your phone, or the other way round.

TVs AND MEDIA PLAYERS
Turn on DLNA in Advanced settings and smart TVs, VLC and Kodi on the same network can find your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works only on your own Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot. Nothing goes over the internet.
• No account, no cloud, no ads.
• Add a PIN so only people you tell can open your share.
• See who is connected, rename or block devices, and stop sharing with one tap.

DirectServe is open source (Apache 2.0) and available in 26 languages.

Note: speed depends on your Wi-Fi or hotspot. Some apps block screen and sound capture.

## What's new in 1.0.27 (500 max)

• A simpler app: choose what to share, tap Start, scan the code.
• Browser home like a streaming app, with Continue watching.
• Add files in one step; the browser updates by itself.
• Wide videos turn the phone sideways in full screen.
• New app icon.
• Mirror screen sound now stays in step with the picture.
• Long sharing sessions no longer stop after a few hours.
• Crash fixes for a busy port and Live Screen refresh.
• Lower memory use with large photos, and a smaller download.
